Four things: the property address, a short description of the scope, your plan set (PDF is fine), and your deadline. From those we send back a fixed fee and a GC authorization letter to put us on record for the filing.

Flat fee per project, quoted and approved before we file — not hourly. The range depends on project size and how demanding the jurisdiction is. Government plan-check and permit fees are separate and paid to the AHJ; we quote and pay them on your behalf when you approve.
